# Term Definitions 用詞定義

## 優待機票使用者 Staff ticket users

- 定義: 現職或曾為本公司服務而退休或退職 (下稱「退休 (職)」) 之本人於人事資料系統已登載有效之眷屬及現職員工登錄之 Travel Mate 同行者，且未因故停權者。
- Definition: Those who are currently employed or retired (hereinafter referred to as "retired employees”), their registered dependents, and their registered companions in the personnel system and those whose rights of staff tickets are not suspended for any reason.

## 現職員工 Current employees

- 定義: 全職及時薪員工，不含國內定期契約人員、實習生及國外雇用臨時人員；外籍機師或另有約定者，依其規定辦理。
- Definition: Full-time and hourly-rated employees excluding domestic fixed-term employees, interns, and foreign temporary employees; foreign pilots or employees with other agreements shall be in accordance with their agreements.

## 眷屬 Dependents

- 定義: 優待機票使用者在職時於人事資料系統登錄之法定配偶、父母及子女；退休 (職) 後不可再新增登錄或更換他人；若有異動請確實更新人事資料。
- Definition: Staff ticket users could register their legal spouse, parents and children in the personnel information system when they are employed. New registration or replacement are not allowed after retirement. If there are any changes, please ensure to update the personnel information system.

## 優待機票 Staff ticket

- 定義: 本公司免費機票 (下稱 ID00 機票) 及有價優待機票皆屬之。
- Definition: The company offers free air tickets (hereinafter referred to as ID00 air tickets) and discounted air tickets.

## CIZED 票 CIZED ticket

- 定義: 本公司原經濟艙有價優待機票 (Y / ID90)。
- Definition: The company's economy cabin ticket with a discount (Y / ID90).

## 有價機票 Revenue ticket

- 定義: 有價優待機票之簡稱，即 CIZED、C / ID90、ID75、ID50、聯航 ZED 等票種，其折扣費率均比照 ZED 合約公告之各別里程費率。
- Definition: The abbreviation of the discounted revenue ticket, that are, CIZED, C / ID90, ID75, ID50, interline ZED and other ticket types. The discount rates are in accordance with the mileage rates announced in the ZED agreements.

## R1 / R2

- 定義: 優待機票使用者私人旅行 (1 為可訂位，2 為空位搭乘)。
- Definition: Discounted tickets for personal travel (1 is confirmed, 2 is standby).

## 點數 Points

- 定義: 本公司按年資核予優待機票使用者 ID00 機票之依據【參照 3.2.2.】，點數給予後一年內 (含留職停薪) 有效。
- Definition: The ID00 ticket entitlement is according to seniority (refer to 3.2.2.), and the points are valid for 1 year after granting (including the period of suspension without pay).

## 進點日 Point starting date

- 定義: 即年資起算日，依進公司日期起算，遇留停後復職者，下一年度進點日則依留停日數予以遞延。
- Definition: The beginning of seniority date. Those who take suspension without pay, the point starting date of next year will be deferred according to the days of leave.

## 機票點 (次) 數效期 Ticket points (times) validity period

- 定義: 點數給予後一年 (含留停) 有效。時間計算皆以台北時間 (UTC+8) 為準。
- Definition: Points are valid within 1 year after granting (including the period of suspension without pay). The validity period is based on GMT (UTC+8).

## 空位搭乘 Seat available

- 定義: 除 ID50 / ID00R1 可訂位外，持 ID00R2、C / ID90、ID75、CIZED 及聯航 ZED 機票者，需於班機有空位且符合酬載限制時，始能搭乘，不得預訂確認機位。
- Definition: Except for ID50 / ID00R1 reservations, ID00R2, C / ID90, ID75, CIZED, and interline ZED tickets will be accepted for boarding when there are seats available on the flight and meet the loading restrictions. Confirmed reservation is not available.

## C / PY (W) / Y 艙 C / PY (W) / Y cabin

- 定義: 商務艙 / 豪華經濟艙 / 經濟艙。
- Definition: Business cabin / premium economy cabin / economy cabin.

## 一張 (次) 機票 One ticket

- 定義: 每個票號計為一張 (次)，開立方式可選擇「單程」、「單點來回」或「A 進 B 出」，最多兩個直飛航段，轉機行程機票亦同，但不可開立兩段相同站點出發之單向行程。
- Definition: Each ticket number is regarded as a ticket. It can be "one-way", "round trip” or "multi-city", up to 2 direct sectors. The connecting itinerary tickets are the same, but it is not allowed to issue two one-way tickets with the same departure port.

## 轉機 Transfer

- 定義: 轉機台北 (XTPE) 或其他站點之優待機票開立，搭機前應先確認後段轉機航班客票情形，除須以最近航班再次離境，並不可以從事非轉機目的行為，若 24 小時內仍無空位航班，則後半段機票無效需另行開票使用。
- Definition: It is necessary to check the loading of the connecting flight if transferring at Taipei (XTPE) or other ports. Except for taking the connecting flight, the non-transfer behavior is not allowed. If there is no seat available within 24 hours, the connecting sector will be invalid, and it is necessary to issue a new ticket.

## 機票系統 Ticket system

- 定義: 指本公司「員工優待機票及候補登錄系統」。
- Definition: It refers to the company's "Staff Ticket and Standby Registration System".

## 核准單編號 Approval Reference Number

- 定義: 因故無法於機票系統開立機票情形，請向人力處聯絡並經同意取得開票核准單編號，可憑此編號經由各分公司票務櫃檯臨櫃方式人工開立機票，機票期限以開立核准號當日起算。
- Definition: If the ticket system is unable to issue the ticket, please contact the HR Department and obtain the approval reference number. Users can use this approval reference number to issue tickets manually at the ticket counter. It is effective from the issue date of approval reference number.

## 曆年制 Calendar year

- 定義: 每年一月一日至十二月三十一日止 (以台北時間為主)。
- Definition: January 1 to December 31 (based on Taipei time).

## 客滿 Loading

- 定義: 一般客票 (含各類已確定機位之可訂位員工優待機票) 於關檯時已客滿為依據。
- Definition: The loading is based on loading all revenue passengers (including all types of reserved staff tickets with confirmed seats) after closing the check-in counter.

## 自助 / 網路報到 Self check-in / online check-in

- 定義: 自行於機場自助報到機 (CUSS) 或使用電腦、手持裝置網路線上報到。
- Definition: Check-in by using Common-Use Self-Service (CUSS) machine at airport or online check-in by using a portable device.

## OFF-LINE STATION

- 定義: 本公司未自行營運之航點。
- Definition: A port not operated by the company.

## TPM

- 定義: Ticketed Point Mileage 實際搭乘航段之哩程。
- Definition: Ticketed Point Mileage is the mileage of the actual flight sector.

## DOJ

- 定義: Date Of Join 進公司日 (於候補排序使用)。
- Definition: Date Of Join (using for standby priority).
